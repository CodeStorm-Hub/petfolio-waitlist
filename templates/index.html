Why PetFolio

Built around
your pet,
not around features.

We started because every other app asked you to adapt to it. PetFolio learns your pet's routines and puts the things that matter front and centre.

  • 🔥

    Streak-based care tracking

    Duolingo-style daily quests make feeding, walking and vet reminders feel rewarding rather than guilt-inducing.

  • 📊

    Smart health timeline

    Every vaccination, vet visit and medication logged in one beautiful timeline — shareable with your vet in seconds.

  • 🤝

    Neighbourhood pet network

    Find nearby pet owners, organise playdates and build real friendships around your mutual love for animals.

  • 🛒

    Profile-aware marketplace

    Recommendations filtered by your pet's species, breed, age and dietary needs — not just generic bestsellers.
